Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to You Revise. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how You Revise ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the You Revise application.

By using You Revise, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

In short: We only collect and use information that is necessary to provide, maintain and improve You Revise. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use You Revise, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Account information required to provide the service
  • Authentication information

Learning Information

When you use You Revise, information relating to your use of the educational features may be stored, including:

  • Questions and answers
  • Quiz results
  • Revision activity
  • Subjects and topics selected
  • Progress and usage information

Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for the application to function properly, such as:

  • Device and operating-system information
  • Application version
  • Basic diagnostic information
  • Information relating to errors or crashes

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Providing You Revise's educational features
  • Generating personalised revision content
  • Generating quizzes and educational responses
  • Saving your revision history and progress
  • Enforcing usage limits associated with your plan
  • Processing and managing subscriptions
  • Improving the application
  • Detecting and preventing misuse
  • Maintaining application security
  • Fixing technical problems and errors

4. Artificial Intelligence

You Revise uses artificial intelligence to provide educational features, including generating explanations, questions, quizzes and other revision content.

Information that you submit to AI-powered features may be processed by the third-party AI service used by You Revise in order to generate a response.

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

5. Password Security

Where passwords are used, You Revise does not intentionally store passwords in plain text. Passwords are processed using cryptographic hashing before being stored.

Despite our security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

You Revise may offer paid subscription plans.

Payments and subscriptions made through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Information relating to your subscription may be made available to the application so that we can determine your subscription status and provide the features associated with your plan.

7. Data Storage

Information associated with your account and use of You Revise may be stored using secure databases and cloud services.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We may share information with service providers where this is necessary to operate You Revise. These providers may include:

  • Cloud and database service providers
  • AI service providers
  • Payment and subscription providers
  • Analytics or diagnostic providers, where applicable

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide You Revise and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymise it, subject to legal, security or operational requirements.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request a copy of certain information

If you wish to exercise any applicable rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

You Revise is an educational application intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements applicable in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

12. Third-Party Services

You Revise may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may have their own privacy policies governing how information is processed.

Third-party services may include Apple's App Store and services used to provide AI, hosting, databases, analytics, authentication or other application functionality.

13. Security

We use reasonable security measures designed to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and should notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorisation.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to You Revise, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us.
