Welcome to You Revise. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how You Revise ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the You Revise application.

By using You Revise, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on how you use You Revise, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Email address

Learning Information

  • Questions and answers

  • Revision activity

Technical Information

  • Creating and managing your account

  • Saving your revision history and progress
  • Enforcing usage limits associated with your plan
  • Processing and managing subscriptions
  • Improving the application
  • Detecting and preventing misuse
  • Maintaining application security
  • Fixing technical problems and errors

4. Artificial Intelligence

You Revise uses artificial intelligence to provide educational features, including generating explanations, questions, quizzes and other revision content.

Information submitted to AI-powered features may be processed by the third-party AI service used by You Revise to generate a response.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

5. Password Security

Where passwords are used, You Revise does not intentionally store passwords in plain text. Passwords are processed using cryptographic hashing before being stored.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

You Revise may offer paid subscription plans.

Payments and subscriptions made through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

7. Data Storage

Information associated with your account and use of You Revise may be stored using secure databases and cloud services.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate You Revise, including cloud, database, AI, payment and diagnostic providers.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide You Revise and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
